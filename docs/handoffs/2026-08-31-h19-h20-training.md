failed. H19 therefore trained no all-source adapter. Its result fingerprint is

## H20 development mechanism and training result

The H19 paired rows showed that four of the five control-only losses came from invalid LoRA output.
Two of the three LoRA-only gains repaired invalid control output. H20 froze a narrow runtime rule:
retain every valid menu-free control output; query the repair adapter only after an invalid control;
use the adapter result only when it is valid.

This rule preserves every correct control by construction because a correct control is valid. The
source-held-out H19 readback under the frozen H20 rule reached 25/38 (65.79%), with two repairs and
zero control-only losses. All six sources were nonnegative. Each of the six synthetic-retention
readbacks preserved parent accuracy and raised valid-output rate from 41.67% to 58.33%. Every H20
development gate passed.

H20 then trained one independent all-source adapter from the unchanged parent using 221 records:
38 historical rows repeated four times plus 69 balanced H16 synthetic replay rows. Training used
the fixed final checkpoint after 96 microsteps and 24 optimizer updates. It took 194.63 seconds and
peaked at 6.05 GB.

## Evidence boundary and next action

H20 is retrospective historical development evidence. Its two source-held-out repairs occur in one
source, and the paired McNemar p-value is 0.5. The all-source smoke test is resubstitution. These
results support keeping the runtime as a development candidate; they do not establish external
capability or justify champion replacement.

The later runtime audit found three limits in the historical H20 report. The smoke test called the
adapter on all 38 cases and applied the guard afterwards, whereas the exact two-stage runtime would
have called the repair adapter on only the nine invalid controls. Sixteen E2 cases also used
different prompts for control and adapter, leaving 22 prompt-comparable historical cases. Finally,
the reported `in_catalog_prediction_rate=1.0` counted empty strings as catalog predictions; the
correct rate is 29/38. This metric defect does not change accuracy or the H20 development gate, but
the historical smoke cannot prove the exact E5 call policy.

## E5 master protocol and source-unavailable outcome

The exact E5 runtime and one-shot external gate were implemented and frozen before any E5 model
output was opened. The model caller receives only case ID, prompt messages, and decoding settings;
gold labels never cross the call boundary. Control calls are completed before the repair adapter is
loaded, and repair is called only for parser-invalid controls. Atomic ledgers write a `started`
receipt before each call and fail closed after an interrupted call rather than calling the model a
second time. The terminal verifier requires intact private details, and the child/data verifier
recomputes source identity, snapshot, complete frame, overlap, coverage, method concentration, and
all fingerprints.

The frozen master requires one source selected before opening with at least 150 eligible cases,
eight methods, 80% catalog coverage, and no method above 40%. Evaluation requires at least 12
invalid-control opportunities over three methods, 50 valid-control identity cases, six
candidate-only gains, six net improvements, a four-point accuracy gain, exact McNemar `p <= 0.05`,
and zero control-only losses. Passing would support only a narrow claim for this exact runtime on
one source; it cannot change the champion or authorize release.

## H22 invalid-control catalog fallback

labels. Three source cases shared one identical question template and emitted `t-test`; the second
template emitted `sign-test` or `sign`. New adapter training remained unauthorized because these
are only two independent templates and two methods. H22 instead preregistered a composition of two
already established mechanisms: preserve the sealed H21 parent control, and only when that control
is invalid call the unchanged parent once with H7's fixed global 28-method catalog prompt.

The contract counted the three repeated independent-group cases as one evidence template. It
required all valid controls to remain identical, zero control-only losses, at least three gains,
three source gains, three valid fallbacks, and successful repair of both distinct templates and
both gold methods. The four fallback calls returned `wilcoxon_signed_rank` once and
`independent_t` three times. All were valid and correct.

The guarded score rose from 19/24 correct and 20/24 valid to 23/24 correct and 24/24 valid. Paired
counts were four candidate-only gains, zero control-only losses, and net +4. Gains covered three
sources, two methods, and both independent question templates. The terminal state is
`SUPPORTED_DEV_COMPOSITION`; all frozen development gates passed. The exact McNemar p-value is
0.125, so this small opened-source result is not statistically distinguishable and remains
development-only.

H22 opened no new source, made no menu-free control calls, trained no weights, and changed neither

differences. E6 does not train weights.

eight methods, 80% coverage, and no method above 40%. A metadata-only child-freeze transition now

templates, with at least 75 valid identity cases and frozen concentration limits. A pass requires

valid-fallback precision of at least 80%, and at most one valid but wrong fallback. A pass would
support only this runtime on this one source; it would not authorize training, promotion, release,
or publication.
